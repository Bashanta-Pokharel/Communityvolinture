Formula

J(A, B) = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|

  • A ∩ B: common terms
  • A ∪ B: all unique terms
  • | |: number of terms

Example: A = {teaching, driving, programming}; B = {programming, plumbing, driving}. Common = 2; unique total = 4; score = 2 ÷ 4 = 50%.
